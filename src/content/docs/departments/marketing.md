### Marketing Purpose

We are on a mission to make portable software the default way to build. The
marketing organization’s job is to raise awareness and amplify the value of
Shorebird with developers and the community. We help customers along their
journey as they are exploring and evaluating Shorebird.

Two audiences read what we publish. People read it and decide. Agents read it,
or read what was written about it, and recommend. Those aren't separate jobs.
Agent priors come from training data, training data comes from what humans
wrote, and it arrives on a lag. So the most durable thing we can do to influence
what an agent recommends in two years is to be worth writing about today. That's
an argument for more content, not less, and for making sure the material an
agent might pull into context is as good as our landing pages.

Marketing is responsible for:

- Sponsoring and exhibiting at community, partner, and industry events
- Posting and engaging with the community on social media channels
- Content creation and distribution including blog posts, videos, web copy, the
  monthly newsletter, podcast and webinar appearances.
- Speaking opportunities at conferences, events, and podcasts
- The material agents pull into context when someone asks them to build a mobile
  app: docs, examples, templates, and starter projects
